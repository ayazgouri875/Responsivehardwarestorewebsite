# KHATRI PLYWOOD & HARDWARE - Website Features

## Business Details
- **Name:** KHATRI PLYWOOD & HARDWARE (खत्री प्लाईवुड & हार्डवेयर)
- **Location:** Borkhera, Kota, Rajasthan, India
- **Contact:** +91 99294 49401

## Features Implemented

### 1. Sticky Header Navigation
- Fixed navigation bar with smooth scroll
- Responsive mobile menu with hamburger icon
- "Get Quote" and "Call Now" buttons
- Bilingual branding (English & Hindi)

### 2. Hero Section
- Full-screen hero with hardware store imagery
- Compelling tagline: "Your Trusted Partner for Quality Plywood & Hardware"
- Prominent call-to-action buttons
- Location display with icon
- Smooth fade-in animations

### 3. About Us Section
- Business overview and trust messaging
- Statistics showcase (Years of Service, Happy Customers, Quality Products, Customer Satisfaction)
- Animated cards with hover effects
- Bilingual content

### 4. Products Section
- Four main product categories:
  - Premium Plywood (BWP & BWR Grade, Termite Resistant, ISI Certified)
  - Designer Laminates (1000+ Designs, Matt & Glossy Finish)
  - Hardware Tools (Power Tools, Hand Tools, Measuring Tools)
  - Construction Materials (Adhesives, Nails & Screws, Paints & Varnish)
- Image cards with hover animations
- Feature lists for each category

### 5. Why Choose Us Section
- Six key benefits:
  - High-Quality Materials
  - Affordable Pricing
  - Trusted by Locals (15+ years)
  - Wide Variety
  - Expert Guidance
  - Quality Assurance
- Icon-based cards with smooth transitions

### 6. Testimonials Section
- Auto-playing carousel slider
- Customer reviews with 5-star ratings
- Bilingual testimonials (English & Hindi)
- Responsive design (3 slides on desktop, 1 on mobile)

### 7. Contact Section
- Contact form that integrates with WhatsApp
- Business hours display
- Embedded Google Maps location
- Contact information cards with icons
- Form fields: Name, Phone, Message

### 8. Footer
- Business information
- Quick navigation links
- Product categories list
- Contact details
- Social media icons (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter)

### 9. Interactive Features
- **WhatsApp Floating Button:** Fixed button with pulsing animation for instant messaging
- **Get Quote Modal:** Popup form with product selection and quantity input
- **Scroll to Top Button:** Appears after scrolling down
- **Smooth Scrolling:** Throughout the entire page

### 10. Design Elements
- **Color Scheme:** Warm wood-inspired colors (Brown #6B4423, Beige #8B6F47, Dark Green accents)
- **Typography:** Clean, professional fonts with bilingual support
- **Animations:** Smooth hover effects, fade-ins, and scale transitions using Motion (Framer Motion)
- **Responsive Design:** Fully responsive for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- **Icons:** Lucide React icons for modern, clean iconography

## Technical Stack
- **Framework:** React 18.3.1 with TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4
- **Animations:** Motion (motion/react)
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Carousel:** React Slick
- **Build Tool:** Vite

## User Journey
1. Visitor lands on hero section with immediate CTA
2. Learns about the business in About section
3. Explores product categories
4. Understands benefits in Why Choose Us
5. Reads customer testimonials
6. Contacts via form, WhatsApp, or phone
7. Can request quote via modal at any time

## SEO & Performance
- Semantic HTML structure
- Optimized images from Unsplash
- Fast loading with Vite
- Accessible navigation
- Mobile-first responsive design

## Key Interactions
- Click "Call Now" → Direct phone call
- Click "Get Quote" → Opens quote request modal
- Submit forms → Opens WhatsApp with pre-filled message
- Click WhatsApp button → Opens chat with business
- Click navigation → Smooth scroll to section
- View testimonials → Auto-rotating carousel

All features are production-ready and optimized for user experience!
